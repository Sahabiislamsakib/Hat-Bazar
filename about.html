Our Story

Hat Bazar was founded in 2020 with a simple vision: to make quality products accessible to every household in Bangladesh. What started as a small online store has grown into one of the most trusted e-commerce platforms in the country.

Our name "Hat Bazar" (হাট বাজার) reflects our roots - traditional Bangladeshi marketplaces where communities gather, connect, and trade. We've brought that spirit online, combining the trust and familiarity of local markets with the convenience of modern technology.

Today, we serve thousands of customers across all 64 districts of Bangladesh, delivering everything from traditional clothing to cutting-edge electronics right to their doorsteps.

Our Mission

To empower every Bangladeshi with access to quality products at fair prices, delivered with reliability and care. We believe that e-commerce should serve everyone, not just those in big cities.

Our Vision

To become Bangladesh's most loved e-commerce platform by 2030, known for our commitment to quality, customer service, and supporting local businesses. We envision a digitally connected Bangladesh where everyone has equal access to goods.
