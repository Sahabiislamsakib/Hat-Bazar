Shipping Questions

We deliver within 2-5 business days for most areas in Bangladesh. Dhaka city deliveries are typically completed within 24-48 hours. For remote areas, delivery may take 5-7 business days.

↩️ Returns & Refunds

We offer a 7-day return policy for most items. Products must be unused, in original packaging, and with all tags attached. Electronics may have different return conditions - please check individual product pages.

To return an item, go to "My Orders" in your account, find the order, and click "Return Item". Follow the instructions to schedule a pickup. Our team will collect the item from your address.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. The refund will be credited to your original payment method or bKash account.

Items that cannot be returned include: innerwear, cosmetics, perishable goods, customized products, and items marked as "Final Sale".
